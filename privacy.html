Privacy Policy

MUSA MOTO RACER · Last updated 2 September 2026

Musa Moto Racer does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

What the game stores

The game saves your progress so you can pick up where you left off:

  • Coins, bikes and landscapes you have unlocked, and your upgrade levels
  • Your best distance and the top-10 leaderboard
  • The name you type for the leaderboard, if you enter one
  • Your settings: graphics, volume, tilt steering, vibration

All of it is written to your device's own local storage. It never leaves the device, it is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app, or using the Reset Progress button in Settings, deletes it permanently.

Network access

The Android app contains the entire game, including the 3D engine, inside the installed package. It works with no internet connection and makes no network requests while you play. The app requests the INTERNET permission only because it is a standard part of the framework the app is built with; the game itself does not use it.

If you play the browser version, your browser contacts GitHub Pages to download the game files, exactly as it would for any website. GitHub's own logging of that request is covered by GitHub's privacy statement.

Permissions

  • Vibration — brief feedback on crashes and pickups. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Motion sensors — only if you enable tilt steering, and only to read the tilt angle while riding. Nothing is recorded or sent.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
